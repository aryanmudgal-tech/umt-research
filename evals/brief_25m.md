# Brief: 25 m bridge beam

Analyze the professor's bridge beam:

- Simply supported: pinned at one end, roller at the other.
- Span: 25 m.
- Elastic modulus: E = 30 GPa.
- Second moment of area about the bending axis: Iz = 0.005 m^4.
- Loading: uniform distributed load q = 30 kN/m acting downward in y over
  the full span.

Report the midspan deflection, the maximum bending moment, and the support
shear forces.
